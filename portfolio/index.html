About Me

I'm a third-year Artificial Intelligence and Data Science student at N.M.A.M Institute of Technology, based in Mangalore, Karnataka. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision and applied machine learning — I like taking a model from a research idea to something a person can actually interact with. Most of my recent projects involve reading signals from images and video: recognizing hand gestures, detecting safety equipment, and predicting disease outbreak risk from environmental data. I care as much about clear documentation and reproducible pipelines as I do about model accuracy. Outside of coursework, I'm organized, adaptable, and comfortable working in teams under deadlines.
